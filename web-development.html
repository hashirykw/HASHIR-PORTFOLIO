What I build

Most projects are one of these four, or a mix of them.

01

Company and marketing sites

Multi-page sites with the pages a business actually needs: services, pricing, team, contact, and a way to capture leads that lands somewhere you can read it.

02

Web apps

Logins, roles, records, search and reporting. Built when a spreadsheet has stopped being enough, like a stock and orders system or a signals dashboard.

03

Admin panels

The back office for the site: edit your own projects, prices, posts and images without opening code or waiting for me.

04

Landing pages

One page built around a single action, loading fast on a phone, ready for an ad campaign to point at.

How it runs

Four steps, and you see something real at the end of each.

Scope

We agree what the site has to do, how many pages, what has to be editable, and what you already have — logo, copy, photos.

Design

You see the real pages, not a mockup in a slide. Changes happen at this stage, where they are cheap.

Build

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or a framework where the project earns one. Fast on a phone, works without JavaScript where it matters, and accessible.

Launch and hand over

Deployed on your own domain with analytics and search setup, plus a short written guide so you can edit it yourself.

Questions

How long does a website take?

A landing page is usually a few days. A multi-page company site is one to three weeks depending on how fast copy and images arrive. A web app with logins and a dashboard is scoped separately, because the timeline follows the features rather than the page count.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. Anything you will want to change often is built to be editable, either through an admin panel or a single content file, and you get a written guide for it.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Most communication is over WhatsApp and email, and I have worked with companies in several countries. Karachi is where I am, not who I work for.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes. Sites usually go on Vercel with your own domain, SSL included, and I set up Search Console and analytics at launch.

How it runs

Four steps, and you see something real at the end of each.

  • Scope — We agree what the site has to do, how many pages, what has to be editable, and what you already have — logo, copy, photos.
  • Design — You see the real pages, not a mockup in a slide. Changes happen at this stage, where they are cheap.
  • Build — Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or a framework where the project earns one. Fast on a phone, works without JavaScript where it matters, and accessible.
  • Launch and hand over — Deployed on your own domain with analytics and search setup, plus a short written guide so you can edit it yourself.

Questions

How long does a website take?

A landing page is usually a few days. A multi-page company site is one to three weeks depending on how fast copy and images arrive. A web app with logins and a dashboard is scoped separately, because the timeline follows the features rather than the page count.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. Anything you will want to change often is built to be editable, either through an admin panel or a single content file, and you get a written guide for it.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Most communication is over WhatsApp and email, and I have worked with companies in several countries. Karachi is where I am, not who I work for.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes. Sites usually go on Vercel with your own domain, SSL included, and I set up Search Console and analytics at launch.

Have a site in mind?

Tell me what it needs to do and I will come back with a scope, a timeline and a number.

About

Web developer, designer and video editor in Karachi

I am Muhammad Hashir — M. Hashir, or hashirykw online. I build custom websites, web apps and admin panels, design logos and brand identities, cut promo videos and reels, and run social media for brands that want to grow. I work from Karachi, Pakistan, with clients around the world.

I founded Nexlyr Solutions, a digital agency covering web development, brand identity, video editing and digital marketing, and InfiniMind Academy, an online platform that teaches AI and coding to children. Before Nexlyr I delivered 51+ projects through MHW Web Solutions, worked with more than 55 freelance clients, ran social media for two companies as head of social media, and worked as head graphic designer for companies in several countries.

How it runs

You approve the shape before the polish, so changes stay cheap.

  • Material and goal — Footage, screen recordings, logo files and a clear idea of what the viewer should do at the end.
  • Structure — A rough cut with the order, the pacing and the on-screen text in place, before any effects.
  • Motion and grade — Graphics, transitions, colour and sound, mixed so it is legible with the sound off, which is how most people will watch it.
  • Delivery — Exported for each place it will run — vertical for reels and stories, landscape for YouTube and sites — with captions where they help.

Questions

What do you need from me to start?

The raw footage or screen recordings, your logo in vector if you have it, and one line on what the video is for. If there is no footage, we talk about what can be built in motion graphics instead.

How long is the turnaround?

A single reel is usually two to four days. A longer explainer with custom motion graphics takes about a week, depending on how much has to be animated from scratch.

Can you match a style I already post in?

Yes. Send two or three videos you like and I will match the pacing, type treatment and colour rather than inventing a new look.

Do you write the script too?

I can shape and tighten one. For explainers I usually rewrite the on-screen text so it reads at the speed people actually watch.

Got footage sitting unused?

Send it over with what you want it to do, and I will tell you what the strongest cut looks like.

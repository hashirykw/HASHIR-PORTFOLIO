How it runs

A month is the smallest useful unit of work here.

  • Audit — What the page has published, what performed, and what the account is actually competing against.
  • Formats — Three or four repeatable formats with a defined look, so the feed reads as one brand.
  • Production and posting — Content made and scheduled ahead, with room kept for anything timely.
  • Review — What moved, what did not, and the change to make next month.

Questions

Do you post as well, or only make the content?

Both. Most clients want the content made and published on a schedule, with approval on anything sensitive before it goes out.

Which platforms?

Instagram first, since that is where most of this work lives, plus Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn where the audience is actually there.

Can you work with our existing designer?

Yes. I can take the strategy and calendar and hand production to your team, though it usually costs less to keep it in one place.

How quickly do results show?

Reach moves within weeks; followers and enquiries take longer. Anything promising otherwise is guessing, and the first month is mostly about finding the formats that work for your audience.
